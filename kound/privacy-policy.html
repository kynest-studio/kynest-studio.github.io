Privacy Policy

This policy explains how information is handled when you use kound.

Effective Date: July 27, 2025

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Kynest Studio (“we”, “us”, or “our”) handles user data in the mobile app “Kound” (“the app”). We are committed to protecting your privacy and minimizing data collection.

2. Information We Collect

We do not directly collect or store personal information from users. However, the app may use third-party services such as advertising providers (e.g., Google AdMob), which may automatically collect certain information such as device identifiers and usage data for the purpose of delivering advertisements.

The app does not access your:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Location
  • Contacts
  • Photos or media

3. Purchases

If you make a one-time purchase to unlock content or features, the transaction is processed securely via Google Play or Apple App Store. We do not collect or store any payment or billing information. All financial data is handled entirely by the platform provider.

4. Data Storage

The app operates offline and stores all usage data locally on your device. We do not upload, sync, or transmit your data to external servers.

5. Children's Privacy

The app is intended for users aged 13 and older. If a minor uses the app, a parent or guardian may need to supervise use or approve any purchases.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy at any time. If changes are made, the updated version will be posted in the app or on our official website. Your continued use of the app indicates acceptance of any changes.

7. Advertising

The app uses third-party advertising services (such as Google AdMob) to display ads. These services may collect certain information automatically, including device identifiers (such as Advertising ID), usage data related to ad interactions, and device/network information.

This information may be used to provide and improve advertising services. We do not directly collect or store this data.
